A game of space and strategy

Docking

Bring your fleet together before your opponent does.

MISSION STATUS

Use the arrow keys to navigate the board and Enter or Space to select a square.

How to play

Two players share the screen. White starts. Select a ship, then a highlighted square. Connect all eight of your ships through horizontal or vertical neighbors to win.

A ship travels in any of eight directions. Count every ship in that direction, including itself; that is the exact travel distance. You may jump your own ships, but not an opposing ship, or land on your own.

You may land on an opposing ship only if it can escape along an empty diagonal for at least four squares. Hover over or focus a ship to see possible displacements: C marks the opponent and E marks its escape squares. Select your ship, then select an opponent. Its captain clicks an escape square before the turn ends. Ships are never captured.

A ship surrounded on all four sides by opposing ships crashes and its fleet loses. A crash takes precedence over docking; if both fleets crash or both connect at once, the moving fleet wins. The same position with the same player to move occurring three times, or a player having no legal move, is a draw. These tie and escape-choice rules are interpretations where the source is silent.
