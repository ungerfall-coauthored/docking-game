A game of space and strategy

Docking

Bring your fleet together before your opponent does.

How to play

Two players share the screen. White starts. Select a ship, then a highlighted square. Connect all eight of your ships through horizontal or vertical neighbors to win.

A ship travels in any of eight directions. Count every ship in that direction, including itself; that is the exact travel distance. You may jump your own ships, but not an opposing ship, or land on your own.

You may land on an opposing ship only if it can escape along an empty diagonal for at least four squares. Its captain chooses an empty square at least four squares away on that diagonal before the turn ends. Ships are never captured.

A ship surrounded on all four sides by opposing ships crashes and its fleet loses. If both fleets connect at once, the moving fleet wins. Three repeated positions or a player with no legal move is a draw. These tie and escape-choice rules are interpretations where the source is silent.
